The True Glow story

When you look great,
you feel great.

Ever felt you open your wardrobe and have nothing to wear? Most of us do. We buy what looks good on someone else — in a store, on Instagram — and wonder why it never feels quite right.

After 12 years in corporate tech and discovering colour consulting in London, I saw the transformative power colour and style has on our whole appearance — and how it makes us feel.

We are all unique. We each have our own colours, our own style, that when we find them — make us truly glow.
